NoManLand is my Ultimate purpose of my life.  
**[NoManLand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_man%27s_land)** refers to the land between borders of two countries, controlled by neither one.  
Its also referred to waste land (which I do not mean) I only took meaning as  
"subject controlled by no one."
  
I have multiple visions with this company. They go beyond the purpose of normal company, i.e., profit maximization.  
I want to open libraries, observatories, laboratories, schools, universities etc for people interested in stellar or interstellar space.  
Regardless of thier race, age (even below 18), income level. All are welcome but if they got that curiosity, interest in that program.  
  
I have one motive, "One from lower income level should not be restricted from the space eduction.".
  
## Who are welcomed:

- Rich with curiosity to learn
- Not so rich but with curiosity to learn
- Anyone with curiosity to learn

## Who are not welcomed:

- Anyone without curiosity to learn, interest in space.

---
There will be no criteria to go inside with money or connections. Only ability and curiosity.  
The libraries will be opened free for everyone which will include all related subjects, novels, fiction, non-fictions related to space.  
  
As you might seeing I'm emphasising "curiosity" very much, because it truly matters.  
I want this company to be non-bloated, clean, transparent.
